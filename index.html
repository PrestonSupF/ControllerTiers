Rules & Guidelines

📌 Quick Rules Overview

  • Every fight is recorded by a Tier Tester.
  • No cheating in any way.
  • You can't refuse screenshares or handcams.
  • Leaderboard placement is 100% score-based.

⚔️ How Tiertesting Works

To request a tiertest, you must join the Discord Server and create a ticket or get somebody to make you a ticket in the #request-test! channel. You will talk with a staff member about which gamemode you would like to test in. You will either fight the tiertester or the tiertester will watch you fight somebody on the tierlist around the skill you are estimated to be, depending on who is available in your region and timezones. If there is nobody available in your region, you will fight in both regions. If your tiertest doesn't occur within a week or you don't respond after making a ticket, it will be closed. Every fight will be recorded or streamed (on YouTube) by the tiertester so the fights can be reviewed if necessary.

📊 Placement Mechanics

  • If you lose to your opponent, you will fight somebody lower than their skill if you aren't already on the list.
  • If you tie or barely beat your opponent, you will be placed in the same tier as them.
  • If you beat your opponent by a significant score difference, you will fight someone in the next tier up. If you lose to them by a large score difference, the previous person you fought will fight someone lower than their tier and possibly be moved down if they lose.
  • If you wish to be removed from the leaderboards or a specific gamemode, you can create a ticket and we will label you as "retired" in that mode with your peak tier displaying.
  • If you feel like you are playing with a distraction or internet problems, you can request to pause or cancel the fight at any time.

🚫 What Counts as Cheating

  • Double clicking in any way, including broken buttons.
  • Autoclicking and macros. This includes hitting with 2 buttons or using trigger modification to hit or place with "full pull" and "soft pull", resulting in double clicks.
  • Using clients or any game modification that gives you a direct advantage with cheats such as reach, timer, velocity, hitbox, killaura, etc. Regular clients such as Flarial, Oderso, and Bedrock Render Dragon are allowed. Cinnabar is not because some servers flag it for cheats.

🔍 If You Are Hackusated

If you are tiertesting and get accused of using a cheat and the tiertester agrees, you will have to prove that you're legit. You'll have to play again with your camera facing your controller while you play, with a CPS display in-game. Depending on the scenario, you may have to share your screen or possibly have your PC checked for hidden cheats.

If your skill significantly drops during the rematch, you will be labeled as a cheater and removed from any leaderboard placement. We are aware of skill adaptation and inconsistencies in rematches, so we will have several people review the footage to determine if you are a legit player. Because autoclicking and double clicking is a less-serious and easy to spot cheat on controller, we will not punish you for using it outside of tier tests. However, being spotted with any other cheat may impact your chances of being on the leaderboards.
