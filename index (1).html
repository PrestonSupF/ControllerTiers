Rules & Guidelines

Review competitive rules for Java and Bedrock gamemodes before requesting fights.

General Rules

  • The fights must be watched and scored by an official tier tester.
  • No cheat clients, proxies, or anything that modifies gameplay directly for an unfair advantage.
  • No boosted sensitivity with any method on Bedrock. Modified deadzones and curves are allowed.
  • You are allowed to remap buttons (like back buttons), but you cannot hit or place with 2 buttons, or use macro configurations to execute multiple inputs simultaneously.
  • If hackusated and a comp hoster agrees to host a screenshare/practical/handcam, you must comply or your fight will be invalidated.
  • Double clicking is not allowed. Use DC prevent in fights or use a controller that doesn't double click.
  • If score ties across regions, the 2 players will share the leaderboard spot.
  • Do not intentionally continue attacking if the opponent is clearly lagging or AFK.
